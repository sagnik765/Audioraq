# Audioraq Launch Platform Plan

Prepared for: Audioraq
Date: April 25, 2026
Primary goal: Launch Audioraq across Product Hunt, Uneed, BetaList, Fazier, Peerlist, LaunchIgniter, TinyLaunch, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, and relevant AI/startup directories in a way that drives real creator activation, not vanity traffic.

## 1. Core Positioning

Audioraq should not launch as "another AI podcast generator." That positioning is too easy to dismiss and too close to the AI-slop problem.

Launch Audioraq as:

> Audioraq is an AI podcast creation studio with quality gates for serious creators.

Short version:

> Audioraq helps podcasters plan, create, quality-check, publish, and grow shows with an AI production team.

The strongest story:

> Most AI tools help people generate content. Audioraq helps podcasters produce publishable shows. It combines AI episode creation, AI Agents quality review, show-first publishing, listener trust signals, and feedback intelligence so creators can ship consistently without lowering quality.

The launch should repeatedly reinforce three ideas:

- Audioraq is creator-first, not a generic podcast directory.
- AI Agents quality gates are a core trust layer, not a side feature.
- Audioraq is built for repeat podcast workflows: show, season, episode, listener feedback, analytics, and improvement.

## 2. Launch Philosophy

The launch should be staggered, not blasted everywhere on the same day.

The right strategy is:

- Validate the story with smaller platforms first.
- Build early backlinks and discovery listings.
- Launch Product Hunt as the main public moment.
- Use builder communities after Product Hunt to collect deeper feedback.
- Keep compounding the listings with testimonials, product updates, and Audioraq Originals.

The lesson from successful Product Hunt launches is that launch platforms are not just traffic machines. Popular products use them to create feedback loops, community proof, and repeated product-market fit learning. Notion and Framer both used Product Hunt/community launches as part of an ongoing relationship with makers, not just a one-day announcement.

## 3. Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch Validation

Timeline: 3 to 6 weeks before Product Hunt.

- Submit to BetaList first because it can have review lead time and is useful for early-adopter validation.
- Recruit 20 to 50 warm supporters who are genuinely interested in podcasting, creator tools, AI workflows, or startup tools.
- Create a private launch doc with supporter instructions: visit, try, comment honestly, share feedback. Do not ask for fake reviews or blind upvotes.
- Run a smoke test across signup, Browse, episode detail, save/queue, Create with AI, AI Agents report, upload, publish, feedback, and analytics.
- Polish at least 5 to 10 proof episodes under Audioraq Originals with strong thumbnails, clean show pages, and visible quality signals.

### Phase 2: SEO And Backlink Wave

Timeline: 1 to 2 weeks before Product Hunt.

- Submit to Fazier, LaunchIgniter, TinyLaunch, Uneed, and relevant AI/startup directories.
- Use consistent UTM-tagged links.
- Use the same core positioning, but adapt the copy per platform.
- Default to free listings unless speed, guaranteed backlinks, or homepage placement are worth the small spend.

### Phase 3: Main Public Launch

Timeline: Product Hunt launch day.

- Launch Product Hunt on Tuesday or Wednesday at 12:01 AM Pacific Time.
- Founder should be online throughout launch day.
- Reply to every meaningful comment.
- Share progress updates on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- Drive visitors to one focused action: sign up as a creator and claim the first 100 creator audit offer.

### Phase 4: Builder Community Wave

Timeline: 1 to 10 days after Product Hunt.

- Launch on Peerlist from a verified individual founder profile.
- Publish an Indie Hackers founder story.
- Use Hacker News Show HN only if the technical story is sharp and the team is ready for blunt feedback.
- Share what was learned from Product Hunt instead of pretending the product is already perfect.

### Phase 5: Post-Launch Compounding

Timeline: 2 to 8 weeks after launch.

- Update listings with milestones, testimonials, product changes, and case studies.
- Publish a launch retrospective.
- Turn user feedback into a public roadmap update.
- Share one creator case study per week.
- Track conversion by source and keep only the channels that produce activated creators.

## 4. Platform-Specific Execution

### Product Hunt

Primary role: flagship launch and social proof.

Recommended timing:

- Tuesday or Wednesday.
- 12:01 AM PT.
- Founder available all day.

Product Hunt positioning:

> Audioraq gives podcasters an AI production team: plan episodes, create audio, run quality gates, publish into shows, and learn from listeners.

Product Hunt tagline options:

- AI podcast studio with quality gates.
- Create better podcasts with an AI production team.
- Plan, create, quality-check, and publish podcasts with AI.

Gallery assets:

- Landing page hero.
- AI Creator Studio intake flow.
- AI Agents scorecard.
- Show > Season > Episode organization.
- Episode detail page with likes, ratings, views, save, queue, and trust signals.
- Creator analytics.
- Feedback agent / business intelligence workflow.

Maker comment:

```text
Hi Product Hunt,

I'm Sagnik, founder of Audioraq.

I built Audioraq because podcasting is still harder than it should be. New creators often have ideas, but they get stuck between blank-page anxiety, recording friction, editing tools, inconsistent audio quality, and weak feedback after publishing.

Audioraq gives podcasters an AI production team:

- Create a show and structure episodes
- Use AI to generate an audio-first podcast draft
- Run AI Agents quality checks for listenability, safety, and improvement feedback
- Publish into a real show/season/episode structure
- Let listeners save, queue, rate, and give feedback

The key idea is not "generate more AI content." The key idea is quality-controlled podcast creation. We are building Audioraq so creators can ship consistently without flooding the internet with low-quality AI audio.

Would love your feedback on:

1. Does the creator workflow feel clear?
2. Would you trust the AI Agents quality report before publishing?
3. What would make you try Audioraq for your own show?

Thank you for checking it out.
```

Rules:

- Do not ask for upvotes.
- Ask people to visit, try the product, leave honest feedback, and comment if they have thoughts.
- Reply like a founder, not a marketing bot.

Primary role: rolling discovery and startup-tool visibility.

Position Audioraq as:

Suggested Uneed description:

### BetaList

Primary role: early-adopter validation before the bigger launch.

Why submit early:

- BetaList is useful for startups seeking early users.
- Review and feature timing may take time.
- It can generate beta signups before Product Hunt.

BetaList angle:

> Audioraq is opening early access for creators who want to launch a podcast with an AI production team.

Offer:

> First 100 creators get a free AI Podcast Studio audit: show positioning, three episode ideas, and one AI-generated episode draft.

Requirements to check before submission:

- Custom domain works: https://www.audioraq.com
- Signup works.
- Landing page explains the product clearly.
- Product is new enough for a beta/startup listing.
- No fake traction claims.

### Fazier

Primary role: startup discovery, launch listing, and backlink value.

Execution:

- Submit Audioraq as SaaS, AI, creator tool, podcasting, and marketing/productivity if categories allow.
- Use the free option if budget matters and adding a badge/backlink is acceptable.
- Consider Lite or Premium only if faster review, homepage visibility, paid promotion, or high-authority backlink value is worth the spend.

Fazier copy:

> Audioraq is an AI podcast creation studio that helps creators plan, create, quality-check, publish, and improve podcast episodes from one workflow.

Launch note:

> Built for podcasters who want an AI production team, not just another content generator. AI Agents reviews quality and safety before episodes reach listeners.

### Peerlist

Primary role: builder and founder community credibility.

Important mechanics:

- Launch from an individual verified Peerlist profile, not only a company profile.
- Complete the project profile to 100%.
- Launch on Monday for maximum weekly visibility.
- Follow no-spam behavior: ask for feedback, not mass upvotes.

Peerlist angle:

> I built Audioraq as an AI-first podcast studio where the interesting part is not generation alone. The interesting part is the workflow: guided creator input, AI Agents quality gates, publishing, listener trust signals, and feedback loops.

Post-launch founder update:

> We launched Audioraq because podcast creation is still fragmented. The first version is focused on helping creators go from idea to quality-checked episode. I would especially love feedback on the Create with AI workflow and whether the AI Agents quality report feels trustworthy.

### LaunchIgniter

Primary role: early-user discovery, weekly launch visibility, and backlink.

Execution:

- Schedule for Monday 00:00 UTC if possible.
- Use the free option by default.
- Consider Basic or Pro only if skipping the waiting period, improving placement, or getting guaranteed backlink value is important.
- Position for early feedback, not just SEO.

LaunchIgniter copy:

> Audioraq gives podcasters an AI production team to plan episodes, create audio drafts, pass quality gates, publish into shows, and learn from listeners.

### TinyLaunch

Primary role: lightweight launch visibility and backlink.

Execution:

- Use the free launch if budget is tight.
- Consider Premium if guaranteed backlink and queue skip are worth it.
- Coordinate a small group of real supporters to visit, try, and comment/review honestly.
- If the platform ranks top launches, focus launch-day activity into the first 24 hours.

TinyLaunch copy:

> Audioraq is an AI-first podcast studio for creators who want better quality control before publishing.

### Indie Hackers

Primary role: founder story and honest build-in-public feedback.

Recommended post title:

> I built an AI-first podcast studio and learned quality gates matter more than generation

Post structure:

- Why podcasting is painful for new creators.
- Why basic AI generation is not enough.
- How Audioraq's Create with AI workflow works.
- What AI Agents checks.
- What is still imperfect.
- What feedback you want from founders and creators.

### Hacker News Show HN

Primary role: technical credibility and blunt product feedback.

Use only if ready for scrutiny.

Show HN angle:

> Show HN: Audioraq - AI podcast creation with quality gates and show-first publishing

What to emphasize:

- Local-first AI roadmap.
- AI Agents quality review.
- Safety/moderation checks.
- Show/season/episode hierarchy.
- Creator workflow automation.
- Honest limitations around AI voice quality and moderation.

What not to do:

- Do not overclaim full factual verification.
- Do not claim the product replaces human creators.
- Do not imply AI content is always high quality.

Activation metrics:

- Visitor to signup conversion.

## 10. Risk Controls

- Do not flood the catalog with low-quality AI episodes before launch.
- Do not overclaim that AI Agents is a full human-level fact checker.
- Do not hide that AI-created episodes are AI-assisted.
- Do not pay for fake reviews, fake upvotes, or fake traffic.
- Do not launch before signup and Create with AI are stable.
- Do not make Product Hunt the only launch bet.
