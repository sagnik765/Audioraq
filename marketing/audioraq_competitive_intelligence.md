# Audioraq Competitive Intelligence Memo

Date: 2026-04-12

## Positioning

Audioraq should not try to out-record Riverside, out-distribute Spotify or Apple Podcasts, or out-video YouTube. The strongest opening is:

**Topic strategy and AI-assisted publishing for podcast creators who want a show-first workflow.**

That keeps the USP intact: Audioraq is not just a hosting shelf. It helps creators decide what to make, package it, publish it, and improve quality before listeners see it.

## Seed Topic Clusters

- AI at work, with practical workflows, team policy, prompt failures, and trust.
- Longevity and health without bro-science, including sleep, strength, recovery, and evidence-based habits.
- Scam culture and digital trust, including deepfakes, privacy, fraud, and verification.
- Creator operations and media business, including show growth, clips, monetization, and audience loops.
- News and culture explainers with receipts, especially business, technology, policy, and internet culture.
- Micro-scenes and identity niches, including diaspora stories, city scenes, hobby economies, and subculture explainers.

## Launch Channels

- Product Hunt: founder-led launch, real demos, and genuine comments. Do not buy upvotes or post fake reviews.
- LinkedIn: founder posts, company-page updates, short case studies, and creator workflow breakdowns.
- Instagram: Reels and carousels that show before-after publishing workflows and short episode clips.
- Creator communities: share useful teardown posts, templates, or launch checklists. Ask for feedback rather than promotion.

## Risks To Avoid

- Fake reviews, fake customers, or fake engagement.
- Spam accounts and mass messaging.
- Reusing copyrighted clips, music, thumbnails, or YouTube content without permission.
- Voice cloning or imitating real people without consent.
- Undisclosed AI-generated media.
- Generic seed content that feels like filler instead of a high-signal demo catalog.
